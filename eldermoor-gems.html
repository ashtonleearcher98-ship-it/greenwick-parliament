Eldermoor Gems

Eldermoor Gems Exhibit The Eldermoor Gems Exhibit is a dazzling showcase of the Greenwick Royal Family’s most prized treasures, unveiled within the serene walls of the Clearwyn Bay Chapel. This stunning collection, officially announced during a royal dinner, features rare and masterfully crafted jewelry, including rings, necklaces, and bracelets, each adorned with exquisite gemstones representing the legacy and elegance of the House of Eldermoor. As the most valuable collection in Greenwick to date, the exhibit offers a glimpse into royal tradition and opulence.
