About the Commonwealth

Welcome to the Commonwealth of Greenwick — an independent micronation founded on the principles of democracy, unity, and civic responsibility. Situated off the coast of Belize, Greenwick is home to a vibrant and growing community of over 14,000 citizens who proudly participate in building a future based on fairness, justice, and opportunity for all.

Our Foundation

The Commonwealth of Greenwick was officially established in 2023 under the leadership of His Majesty King Ashton I , marking the beginning of a new chapter in self-governance and civic innovation. Built on a constitutional framework that blends parliamentary democracy with a ceremonial monarchy, Greenwick upholds the values of freedom, integrity, and social progress.

Government Structure

Greenwick is governed through a

bicameral parliamentary system , comprising

The Monarch , who serves as Head of State and symbol of national unity.

The Prime Minister

, elected by the citizens and responsible for leading the government. The National Assembly , made up of elected representatives who debate and pass legislation. The Judiciary , an independent body that ensures laws are interpreted fairly and justly.

National Vision

Our vision is to create a secure, educated, and environmentally sustainable future for our citizens. We are committed to: Transparency and Accountability in government. Universal Access to Healthcare and Education through programs like MediLink . Cultural Celebration and Diversity , reflecting our island’s unique identity.
