Royal Archives

Royal Archives

The Royal Archives of the Commonwealth of Greenwick safeguard the official historical record of the nation, including its transformation, leadership, and royal legacy. These archives are maintained to preserve national identity, inform future generations, and honor the history of the monarchy and government.

Historical Background

The territory was originally founded in 2023 under the name Veridonia —a community-led region with a vision of independence, sustainability, and unity. At this time, it operated with informal governance and no royal structure. In 2024 , the nation was formally restructured and renamed to the Commonwealth of Greenwick . This transition marked the beginning of a constitutional monarchy , a codified government, and an emphasis on democratic values and citizen representation.

Reign of His Majesty King Ashton I

His Majesty King Ashton I became the first and only reigning monarch of Greenwick upon the nation’s reformation in 2024. As the symbolic Head of State, King Ashton I embodies the spirit of national unity, tradition, and continuity.

Under his reign

Veridonia was renamed and reformed into the Commonwealth of Greenwick The National Charter was adopted The Royal Family and noble titles were established

Diplomatic relations were opened with the Republic of Rondland

Three Prime Ministers have served under the Crown

Prime Ministers of Greenwick

Jakob Furgason – 2023–2025 The first elected Prime Minister of Veridonia (later Greenwick). He led the nation through its foundational years and oversaw the renaming and formation of key institutions. He resigned in 2025 . Rachel Sinclair – 2025–2025 (Assasinated) The second Prime Minister, elected in a democratic vote. Her leadership focuses on transparency, modernization, and international cooperation. In Remembrance: James Hendricksson (1996–2025)

James Hendricksson served as the only unelected Prime Minister in the history of the Commonwealth of Greenwick, assuming office during one of the nation’s darkest and most uncertain moments. Following the tragic assassination of Prime Minister Rachel Sinclair in 2025, Hendricksson—then Deputy Prime Minister —was called upon to lead as Acting Prime Minister. Though he never sought or received an electoral mandate, his leadership was guided by a profound sense of duty to the nation and its democratic institutions. During his time in office, Hendricksson provided continuity, stability, and calm leadership amid national grief and political upheaval. He upheld constitutional norms, safeguarded democratic processes, and ensured a peaceful transition toward the next general election. Remembered for his integrity, restraint, and commitment to the rule of law, James Hendricksson’s tenure stands as a testament to service in crisis.
