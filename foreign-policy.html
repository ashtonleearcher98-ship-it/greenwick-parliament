Commonwealth of Greenwick

Foreign Policy

Foreign Policy of the Commonwealth of Greenwick The Commonwealth of Greenwick pursues a peaceful, principled, and independent foreign policy grounded in respect for sovereignty, international cooperation, and human rights. As a micronation, Greenwick’s foreign affairs are focused on building meaningful diplomatic relationships, promoting national values, and maintaining neutrality in global conflicts.

Core Principles

Greenwick’s foreign policy is guided by the following core values

Peaceful Coexistence Mutual Recognition of Sovereignty Non-Aggression and Neutrality Promotion of Democracy and Human Rights

Environmental Responsibility and Global Sustainability

Key Foreign Policy Goals

Establish Diplomatic Ties Seek formal relations with other micronations and supportive sovereign states. Build embassies, consulates, and cultural missions when appropriate. Mutual Recognition Agreements Promote Greenwick’s legitimacy through mutual recognition treaties. Participate in Global Micronational Forums Engage in discussions on intermicronational cooperation, development, and peacekeeping. Support International Peace Maintain a strict non-aggression stance. Avoid involvement in global conflicts, disputes, or military alliances. Collaborate on Global Issues Partner with allies on climate change, cyber security, and disaster response . Exchange knowledge and solutions with other nations. Protect Citizens Abroad Provide guidance and support for Greenwick passport holders overseas.

Diplomatic Relations

Greenwick currently holds formal diplomatic relations with

The Republic of Rondland (2025 Treaty of Friendship and Cooperation) Additional micronations to be listed upon formal recognition Relations are managed by the

Ministry of Foreign Affairs

, which also oversees international treaties, recognitions, and representation.

International Neutrality

Greenwick remains

Politically non-aligned Not involved in international disputes Dedicated to peaceful conflict resolution The National Defense Force is strictly defensive and does not project force abroad. Cultural & Educational Exchanges

Greenwick promotes cross-border understanding through

Online cultural exchange programs Youth forums and educational webinars Joint environmental and civic initiatives

Conclusion

Greenwick’s foreign policy reflects its identity as a peace-loving, diplomatically active micronation committed to fairness, sovereignty, and shared global progress. By fostering meaningful partnerships and standing firm on its values, Greenwick ensures its voice is heard on the intermicronational stage.
