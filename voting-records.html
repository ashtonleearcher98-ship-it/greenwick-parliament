Voting Records

The Voting Records page provides a transparent and accessible summary of voting decisions made by members of the

Parliament of Greenwick

. It is an essential resource for citizens who wish to follow the legislative decisions that shape the nation’s laws. The page includes detailed information on how members voted on specific bills, resolutions, and key issues.

How It Works

The Voting Records are updated after each voting session in Parliament. They include the names of members, their respective votes (Yes, No, or Abstain), and the final outcome of each vote. Citizens can easily see how their elected representatives have voted on significant pieces of legislation. Accessing Voting Records The Voting Records are organized by bill and session . You can search by bill name, date, or member to see how specific legislation was voted on and track voting trends throughout each Parliament session. Recent Votes

1. Greenwick National Education Act

Date of Vote : April 20, 2025 Bill Status : Conceded Outcome : Approved Vote Summary : This bill is still in debate and has not yet reached the voting stage. It aims to reform the education system, increase public school funding, and standardize curricula across Greenwick.

2. Greenwick Environmental Protection Act

Date of Vote : April 12, 2025 Outcome : Passed Total Votes : Yes: 80 No: 15 Abstain: 5 Vote Summary : The bill aims to set new environmental standards and reduce carbon emissions. It was passed after a lengthy debate and minor amendments to certain clauses related to waste management.

3. National Security Reform Bill

Date of Vote : April 5, 2025 Outcome : Passed Total Votes : Yes: 95 No: 10 Abstain: 5 Vote Summary : This bill addresses national security concerns, including cyber threats, border security, and emergency preparedness. It passed with a strong majority, with few opposing votes.

4. Greenwick Healthcare Expansion Act

Date of Vote 139 : March 28, 2025 Outcome : Passed Total Votes : Yes: 75 No: 30 Abstain: 5 Vote Summary : This bill ensures that healthcare services are expanded nationwide, including funding for rural clinics and mental health services. The bill was passed with some opposition from representatives concerned about the financial burden.

How to View Individual Voting Records

To view how individual members of Parliament voted, simply

Select the bill name from the list above. Click on the “View Voting Details” link. A detailed list of votes will appear, showing the names of members and how they voted. Use the search function to find votes by a specific member or date.

Public Engagement

Greenwickians are encouraged to stay informed about how their representatives are voting. The

Voting Records

page allows citizens to

Follow the legislative process and see where their representatives stand on crucial issues. Engage with their representatives to express support or opposition for particular bills. Ask representatives to explain their votes , especially for contentious or important pieces of legislation. Transparency and Accountability The Voting Records page upholds Greenwick’s commitment to transparency and accountability . By providing easy access to detailed voting information, the public can ensure that elected representatives are acting in the best interest of the citizens and staying true to their campaign promises.
